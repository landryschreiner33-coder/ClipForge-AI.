Turn long videos into short clips, on your own PC

ClipFoundry is a desktop app for Windows. It finds the best moments in a long video and turns them into vertical clips with captions for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels. Your videos stay on your computer. Only the clips you post are uploaded.

Get ClipFoundry on GitHub How it works

There is no ClipFoundry account and no ClipFoundry cloud service: the app runs on your computer and opens in your web browser.

What it does

Finds the best moments

It writes down what is said in your video, on your PC (fast with an NVIDIA graphics card), and picks moments that start strong and end on a payoff.

Makes vertical clips

9:16 framing that follows faces or the person talking, with word-by-word captions in a few clean styles.

Writes titles from the clip

Titles, captions and hashtags are built from the words said in the clip, and a post can also carry a short call to action and a credit line. AI suggestions that add names or numbers not said in the clip are thrown out. These checks match words automatically and can miss things. Text you type yourself is not removed.

You decide what gets posted

Publish to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs after you approve each post, or, for YouTube only, under an automatic-publishing permission you turn on, with a daily limit and posting hours you choose. TikTok always needs your OK on each post. You can also export the clips and post them yourself.

Autopilot, if you want it

It clips the videos you put in your videos folder, and videos covered by an agreement you recorded or by a public domain or Creative Commons Attribution license that the source reports. In normal mode, other discoveries are skipped, and it tells you why. Optional Local test mode also makes local clips from supported public discoveries regardless of reuse status and holds all platform uploads. You are responsible for having the right to reuse each video.

Real numbers only

Results show the views and likes YouTube and TikTok report. Scores are marked as estimates, and nothing is made up.

How it works

VIDEO → TRANSCRIPT → BEST MOMENTS → CLIPS → 9:16 → CAPTIONS → TITLES → CHECK → POST OR EXPORT

  1. Install it and start it. Download ClipFoundry, then double-click start.bat. It opens in your browser at 127.0.0.1, an address only your own computer can reach.
  2. Add a video. Choose a file, or put your videos in your Videos\ClipFoundry folder for Autopilot.
  3. Check the clips. Watch them, trim them or change the captions, then export them.
  4. Post, if you like. Connect YouTube or TikTok, then approve each post, or turn on automatic publishing for YouTube in Autopilot. Before Autopilot posts a clip, ClipFoundry checks the exact final file. A clip you post yourself from its Prepare post page is uploaded when you confirm.

Connecting YouTube and TikTok

  • You sign in on Google's or TikTok's own page. ClipFoundry never sees or stores your password.
  • On YouTube it asks to manage your videos (it only uploads them), to see your YouTube account and to see YouTube Analytics reports. It uses them to upload your posts, to show your channel's name and picture, and to read the status and results of your posts. If you use Autopilot without your own YouTube API key, it also uses your YouTube sign-in to find videos and to check which channel posted them.
  • On TikTok it asks to show your name and picture, to post videos or send them to your TikTok inbox, and to read the results of your posts.
  • Before a TikTok post you choose who can see it, whether people can comment, duet or stitch, and whether it is promotional, and you confirm TikTok's music rules. TikTok always needs your OK on each post.
  • The platforms' own limits apply. For example, until TikTok audits the app, posts sent straight to TikTok can only be seen by you. ClipFoundry tells you so instead of working around it, and can send the video to your TikTok inbox instead, where you finish and post it in the TikTok app.
  • You can disconnect an account at any time, in ClipFoundry or in your Google or TikTok settings.

Read exactly what is stored and sent in the Privacy Policy.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), with 8 GB of memory or more.
  • Python 3.11 or 3.12, and FFmpeg. The setup guide shows how to install both.
  • An NVIDIA graphics card is optional. Without one, transcription works, just more slowly.
  • About 3 GB of free disk space (about 6 GB with an NVIDIA graphics card), plus room for your videos.
  • To post from the app: your own YouTube channel and Google Cloud project, and your own TikTok developer app. Exporting clips needs no account at all.
