Turn long videos into short clips, on your own PC

ClipFoundry is a desktop app for Windows. It finds the best moments in a long video and turns them into vertical clips with captions for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels. Your videos stay on your computer.

Get ClipFoundry on GitHub How it works

There is no ClipFoundry account and no cloud service: the app runs on your computer and opens in your web browser.

What it does

Finds the best moments

It writes down what is said in your video, on your PC (fast with an NVIDIA graphics card), and picks moments that start strong and end on a payoff.

Makes vertical clips

9:16 framing that follows faces or the person talking, with word-by-word captions in a few clean styles.

Writes titles from the clip

Titles, captions and hashtags come from the words said in the clip. Text that adds names, numbers or claims is thrown out.

Posts only with your OK

Publish to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs after you approve each post, or export the clips and post them yourself.

Autopilot, if you want it

It clips the videos you put in your videos folder and videos it may use (public domain, Creative Commons Attribution, or agreements you recorded). Everything else is skipped, and it tells you why.

Real numbers only

Results show the views and likes YouTube and TikTok report. Scores are marked as estimates, and nothing is made up.

How it works

VIDEO → TRANSCRIPT → BEST MOMENTS → CLIPS → 9:16 → CAPTIONS → TITLES → CHECK → POST OR EXPORT

  1. Install it and start it. Download ClipFoundry, then double-click start.bat. It opens in your browser at 127.0.0.1, an address only your own computer can reach.
  2. Add a video. Choose a file, or put your videos in your Videos\ClipFoundry folder for Autopilot.
  3. Check the clips. Watch them, trim them or change the captions, then export them.
  4. Post, if you like. Connect YouTube or TikTok and approve each post. ClipFoundry checks the exact final file before anything goes out.

Connecting YouTube and TikTok

  • You sign in on Google's or TikTok's own page. ClipFoundry never sees or stores your password.
  • It asks only for what posting needs: to upload the videos you approve, to show your account's name and picture, and to read the results of your own posts.
  • Before a TikTok post you choose who can see it, whether people can comment, duet or stitch, and whether it is promotional, and you confirm TikTok's music rules. TikTok always needs your OK on each post.
  • The platforms' own limits apply. For example, TikTok only allows private posts from apps it has not audited, and ClipFoundry tells you so instead of working around it.
  • You can disconnect an account at any time, in ClipFoundry or in your Google or TikTok settings.

Read exactly what is stored and sent in the Privacy Policy.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), with 8 GB of memory or more.
  • Python 3.11 or 3.12, and FFmpeg. The setup guide shows how to install both.
  • An NVIDIA graphics card is optional. Without one, transcription works, just more slowly.
  • About 3 GB of free disk space, plus room for your videos.
  • To post from the app: your own YouTube channel and Google Cloud project, and your own TikTok developer app. Exporting clips needs no account at all.
