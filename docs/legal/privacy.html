Privacy Policy

ClipFoundry · Effective October 1, 2026

The short version

  • ClipFoundry is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. Your videos, clips, transcripts, settings and account connections are stored there. We don't run any server that receives them.
  • We, the publisher, receive nothing from the app: no videos, no account details, no usage statistics and no crash reports. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Information leaves your computer only when you use a feature that needs another service, for example posting to YouTube or TikTok, searching for videos, or an optional AI service you turn on. It then goes straight from your computer to that service.
  • You sign in on Google's and TikTok's own pages. ClipFoundry never sees or stores your passwords.
  • Deleting something in ClipFoundry removes it from your computer. It does not delete a video you already posted on YouTube or TikTok. You do that on the platform.

1. Who this policy is from

ClipFoundry is published by Landry Schreiner ("we", "us"). You can reach us at landryschreiner456@gmail.com. This policy covers the ClipFoundry desktop app and this website.

The app runs on your computer and under your control, so you decide what it processes. We have no access to the information stored in it.

4. YouTube API Services

ClipFoundry uses the YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your information as the Google Privacy Policy describes.

When you connect YouTube, ClipFoundry asks Google for these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload), to upload the posts you approve.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), for your channel's name and picture and the status and statistics of videos posted with ClipFoundry.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly), for watch time and how much of your posts people watched.

ClipFoundry uses this information only on your computer: to publish, to show you status and results, and to plan future posts. YouTube information is not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed: videos Autopilot found but did not use are deleted, and the statistics of videos it used are deleted, while their title, channel and link stay with the clips made from them. Statistics of your own posts are also deleted after 30 days, unless you turn on "Google approved derived metrics" in Settings because Google approved your project to keep them. Then they are kept while your channel stays connected.

You can remove ClipFoundry's access at any time in ClipFoundry (Settings → Accounts → Disconnect) or on your Google Account's third-party connections page.

5. TikTok

When you connect TikTok, ClipFoundry asks TikTok for these permissions:

  • user.info.basic, for your display name and profile picture.
  • video.upload, to send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft that you finish in the TikTok app.
  • video.publish, to post a video you approved straight to your profile (unless you turn this off).
  • video.list, for the statistics of your own videos (unless you turn this off).

ClipFoundry stores your sign-in tokens, your TikTok account ID and profile basics, the posting options TikTok offers for your account, the ID and status of each post, and your posts' statistics. TikTok handles your information as TikTok's Privacy Policy describes. You can disconnect TikTok in ClipFoundry, or remove the app's access in the TikTok app's settings.

6. How your information is protected

  • On Windows, sign-in tokens, API keys and client secrets are encrypted with Windows' own data protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account on that computer can read them. On other systems they are stored without encryption in ClipFoundry's database, which it makes readable only by your user account.
  • ClipFoundry's screens open in your web browser, and by default only your own computer can reach them (the address 127.0.0.1). If you choose to open them to your home network, anyone on that network can use them, so do that only on a network you trust. Connecting accounts and publishing always work only from your own computer.
  • Signing in uses the platforms' official sign-in pages, so your passwords never pass through ClipFoundry.

No software or computer is perfectly secure. Anyone who can use your Windows account can open ClipFoundry and see what it stores, so protect your computer with a password and keep it up to date.

7. Your choices

  • Disconnect an account: Settings → Accounts → Disconnect. ClipFoundry asks the platform to cancel its access and deletes the tokens on your computer. If the platform can't be reached, the tokens are deleted anyway, and you can also remove access on the platform (sections 4 and 5).
  • Stop automatic publishing: turn off the automatic-publishing permission for YouTube in Autopilot. TikTok posts always wait for your OK.
  • Delete a video and its clips: in the Library. This deletes ClipFoundry's copy of the video, its clips, transcript and analysis. The original file in your own folders is not touched. Two kinds of records stay until you delete the data folder: the history of posts that were planned or published (with their results), and a short fingerprint of each published clip, which stops the same clip from being posted twice.
  • Delete everything: close ClipFoundry and delete its data folder. Your videos folder and any files you exported are separate, and stay until you delete them.
  • Optional services: turn off the free-license library, remove a Tavily key, or set AI scoring back to "Local" in Settings → Advanced.

8. Deleting on your computer doesn't delete on YouTube or TikTok

Once a video is posted, a copy belongs to your YouTube or TikTok account. Deleting the clip, disconnecting the account or deleting ClipFoundry does not remove that post. To remove it, delete it in YouTube Studio or in the TikTok app. YouTube and TikTok keep and handle what you posted under their own policies.
