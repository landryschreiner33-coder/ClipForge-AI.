4. YouTube API Services

ClipFoundry uses the YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you connect a YouTube channel (Google OAuth), ClipFoundry requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: upload the videos you approve;
  • youtube.readonly: your channel's name and picture, and the status and statistics of videos you published with ClipFoundry;
  • yt-analytics.readonly (optional): watch time and retention of those videos.

It stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens, your channel ID, name and picture URL, the IDs, upload and processing status, privacy status and statistics of videos it published, and, for discovery, public metadata and statistics of videos returned by YouTube search and chart requests. It uses this data only to publish, to show you status and results, and to plan future posts on your computer. It does not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's Developer Policies require. Metrics derived from YouTube data are computed only if Google approved this for your API project; with that approval, the statistics of your own videos are kept for your results history while your channel stays connected.

5. TikTok

When you connect TikTok (TikTok Login Kit), ClipFoundry requests user.info.basic (your display name, username and avatar), video.upload and/or video.publish (to send or post the videos you approve), and optionally video.list (the statistics of your videos). It stores the OAuth tokens, your TikTok open ID and profile basics, the creator options TikTok returns before a post (privacy options, maximum duration, interaction settings), publish IDs and status, and the statistics of your videos. TikTok's handling of your data is described in TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. When data leaves your computer

  • YouTube / Google and TikTok: the videos and text you approve, and the API requests needed to publish and read results.
  • Optional cloud AI: if you choose a cloud AI provider (for example, the Claude API or an OpenAI-compatible service), excerpts of transcripts and clip text are sent to it to suggest scores and text. Its own privacy terms apply. Local AI (such as Ollama on your computer) sends nothing out.
  • Downloads you start: transcription model downloads (Hugging Face) and URLs you import are fetched from their hosts, which see your IP address like any website.

Nothing is sent to [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME].

7. Security

On Windows, OAuth tokens and API secrets are encrypted with Windows DPAPI so that only your Windows account on your computer can decrypt them. On Linux and macOS they are stored in the local database, which is readable by your user account only. By default, the local web interface only accepts connections from your own computer.

8. Your choices and control

  • Disconnect YouTube or TikTok in ClipFoundry (Settings → Publishing): the tokens are revoked with the platform and deleted.
  • Revoke access from Google at any time: Google Account → Third-party apps & services (also security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • Revoke access from TikTok in the TikTok app's settings for connected apps.
  • Delete projects and clips in ClipFoundry, or delete its data folder to remove everything.
