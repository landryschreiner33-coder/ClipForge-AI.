2. What ClipFoundry is

ClipFoundry is desktop software for Windows. It transcribes your videos, finds strong moments, makes vertical clips with captions, suggests titles and captions, and, if you connect your accounts, publishes the posts you approve to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs. Its optional Autopilot does these steps in the background. ClipFoundry itself runs on your computer. It sends information to other services only for features that need them, such as posting, Autopilot's searches, downloading its speech model or an AI service you turn on, as our Privacy Policy explains. There is no ClipFoundry account and no ClipFoundry cloud service.

8. Automatic suggestions and AI

Transcripts, chosen moments, titles, captions, hashtags, hooks, scores (such as Viral Potential) and posting times are produced automatically, by ClipFoundry's own rules and, if you turn it on, by an AI service. Transcripts can mishear words. ClipFoundry builds suggested text from the words said in the clip, and it can add a short call to action and a credit line. It drops AI suggestions that add names or numbers it can't find in the clip, and it also looks for words that make claims the clip does not make. It does not remove text you type yourself. These checks match words automatically and can still miss mistakes. Scores are estimates, not promises of views, followers, income or any other result. Review every post before you approve it.
