# Download website copy (prepared, not published)

For review before the download site (https://clipfoundry-studio.landryschreiner33.chatgpt.site) is changed. It
describes only what is implemented and tested in this build. Keep the current download link and checksum until a
new build has been released and verified.

**ClipForge** (formerly ClipFoundry) turns long videos into captioned vertical clips, and your clips into an edited
YouTube video.

- **Make clips.** Highlights finds the strongest short moments; Full stories makes one complete vertical clip for each
  story in a long recording, with its setup and ending kept.
- **Make a YouTube video.** Upload your clips, answer four questions and get an edited rough cut you can change and
  render.
- **Your style, remembered.** Saved styles and importable editing skills shape new projects, and every project shows
  exactly which ones it used.
- **Several accounts.** Connect more than one YouTube channel and TikTok account and choose where each project goes.
  TikTok posts always wait for your OK.
- **Your robot office.** A team of robots shows the real work as it happens. Name them whatever you like.

Runs on your computer. A private, sign-in-protected server version is available for people who host their own;
it is not a hosted service.

Not claimed: hosted accounts for other people, a cloud service run by us, guaranteed story detection (story
boundaries are an estimate you can adjust), or any real-world throughput number.
