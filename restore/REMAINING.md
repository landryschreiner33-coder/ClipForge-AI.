# Batch 4 review findings still to fix (7-25)

From a four-reviewer review of batch 4 (1-6 and 26-32 are fixed in 616d071). Each needs a regression test
that fails on the old code (without deleting anything real) and passes after the fix.

7. Make the preview / Export / Write can leave the story stuck forever: the worker can run the job before start() writes job_id (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:426)
   What happens: start() sets status 'voicing' (or 'writing'), then enqueue() hands the job to the idle worker thread, and only then _patch(job_id=...). If the worker claims the job first, _own_job(job) sees the old job_id and returns None. The handler returns {'skipped': True} and the job ends 'done'. The story stays 'voicing'/'writing' with working=true. Stop does nothing (cancel_job only touches queued/running jobs, so _settle never runs). Edits, Preview, Export and Write all answer 409. recover() skips done jobs. Only Delete gets out of it.
   Fix: Write job_id before the worker can see the job: call enqueue(..., submit=False), _patch(job_id=...), then worker.submit_studio(job_id). Or identify ownership by revision (job['revision'] == story['revision']), as shorts.py does with build numbers. Also make stop() settle a story that is in WORKING when its job is no longer queued or running.
8. A ClipForge AI story over 650 words fails the job but leaves the story stuck in 'writing' forever (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:496)
   What happens: write_job calls _clean_script(got['script']) outside its try/except. writer.write_story can return up to 6000 characters. A revision counts as fitting whenever it has 30 words or more, and for a new story the first answer is kept even when it is too long. So an answer over MAX_WORDS (650) raises StoryError. jobs.run() calls fail(), which marks the job failed but runs no stop hook, so _settle never runs. The story keeps status 'writing'. Stop cannot cancel a failed job, edits and new writing get 409, and recover() ignores the job.
   Fix: Run _clean_title/_clean_script inside the try, so a failure patches status='failed' with a plain error. Or have write_story treat more than textstory.MAX_WORDS as not fitting, and cut the result to the limit or raise WriterError. As a general safety net, set the story to failed in jobs.run's failure path (an on_stop-style hook on 'failed').
9. Stop pressed as the narration finishes is overwritten with 'rendering' and the story stays stuck (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:513)
   What happens: After make_narration returns, render_job runs _patch(status='rendering') without checking lease.canceled(). Stop can land after make_narration's last cancel check: during the tone copy when tone is 0, during estimate_words, after Whisper's last segment check, or in words_back. Stop sets the story to draft ('Stopped. Nothing new was kept.'), then this patch sets it back to 'rendering'. render() then raises Cancelled, which jobs.run swallows without calling the stop hook. The story stays working=true and every later Stop, edit or Preview is refused.
   Fix: Before the 'rendering' patch, check lease.canceled() and _own_job(job), and raise Canceled when either fails. Better, make the patch conditional (UPDATE ... WHERE id=? AND job_id=? AND status IN WORKING). Make stop() settle a WORKING story whose job is no longer queued or running.
10. A multi-word pronunciation in the title drops the first story word from the captions (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:564)
   What happens: make_narration stores title_words = len(title_read.split()), which counts written tokens. words_back() merges the tokens of a multi-word rule ('New York' -> 'Noo Yawk') into one entry. story_ass then takes words[:title_words] as the title and so swallows the first script word. That word never appears in the captions, and the title card stays up until that word ends.
   Fix: Count the title entries the way words_back makes them: title_words = sum(1 for c in counts[:len(title_read.split())] if c > 0). Pronounce the title and the script separately so no rule can span the boundary.
11. 'Open in the editor' keeps pointing at an old or deleted editor video after a new export (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:524)
   What happens: open_in_editor stores project_id on the story and export_asset_id inside story.export. A later export replaces the whole export dict (render_job: _patch(**{what: made})) but leaves project_id. Deleting the editor video does not clear it either. The page (TextStories.tsx OpenInEditor) shows a plain link to #/video/<old project_id> whenever project_id is set, and never calls open-in-editor again. So the new export can never be opened in the editor, and after the editor video is deleted the link leads to a 404.
   Fix: Tie the editor project to the export it was made from: store the export key with project_id, and show the link only when it matches the current export and the project still exists (check in view()). Otherwise offer the open-in-editor button again.
12. Earlier Text Story exports are offered and auto-picked as backgrounds once the story is re-exported or deleted (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:298)
   What happens: usable_backgrounds excludes only export_asset_id values found in the current export dict of existing stories. A new export replaces the dict, and deleting the story removes the row, so the asset made for the editor is no longer excluded. Let ClipForge pick then puts a previous story video, with its burned-in title card and captions, behind a new story, against the docstring's 'Never the exports of text stories themselves'.
   Fix: Mark these assets durably, for example a column or table of text-story export asset ids that survives re-export and story deletion, or carry export_asset_id over when a new export is patched, and exclude them in usable_backgrounds.
13. Auto-pick chooses a linked video whose file was deleted or moved, and the preview then fails (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:306)
   What happens: usable_backgrounds skips the existence check for linked assets: `if not uploads.is_linked(a) and not Path(a["path"]).is_file(): continue`. A linked video (the owner's own file used where it is) that was deleted or moved is still listed under From my videos and picked by Let ClipForge pick. _source_args then fails with 'missing from this computer' and the preview or export fails.
   Fix: Check Path(a['path']).is_file() for every asset, linked or stored, before offering or picking it.
14. 'Keep this voice for this story' saves a favourite that nothing ever reads (dead control) (frontend/src/pages/TextStories.tsx:494)
   What happens: The checkbox PUTs a 'project:<story id>' favourite. In the product code the only reader of favourites is _starting_voice, which looks up only style:casual when a story is created; favourite_for(project_id=...) is called nowhere outside the test. On the page, pickStyle uses only the style favourite, so choosing a style that has a favourite still replaces the story's voice even with 'Keep this voice for this story' checked. Ticking the box changes no behaviour at all.
   Fix: Use the story favourite: when it is set, pickStyle and any other voice reset keep its voice, speed and tone, and the backend applies it wherever a story's voice is chosen. Or remove the checkbox.
15. Deleting a Text Story keeps its Brain note, though the privacy page says deleting it removes such memories (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:283)
   What happens: write_story records a Brain episode scoped 'project:<story id>' ('ClipForge AI wrote the text story "<title>" (N words).'). textstory.delete removes the row, its favourites and its folder, but not that memory or its uses. privacy.html (lines 541-549) says memories are kept until you forget them or 'delete the one project or video a memory is kept for'. The Text Stories section (line 485) lists what deleting a story removes and does not mention the note. The story's title stays in the Brain after the story is gone.
   Fix: In textstory.delete, delete brain_memories (and brain_memory_uses) with scope 'project:<story id>', as project_delete does for videos. Or change privacy.html to describe what is kept.
16. 'Hear' audio of the story's opening words is kept in voices/previews after the story is deleted, and the privacy page never mentions it (clipfoundry/makers/textstory_routes.py:201)
   What happens: /api/studio/voices/preview caches each sample as data/voices/previews/<hash>.wav. The page sends the story's first 24 words for 'Hear your story's start' and the style Hear buttons. These recordings of the story's text are never removed, including when the story is deleted. privacy.html documents Hear samples only for animated shorts (shorts/samples) and says deleting a story deletes its narration and files. A related point, from reading the code: with the local voice engine, voice.speak returns an 'original' in engines/made, with a note holding the whole text read. make_narration ignores it, so a copy of the full narration and script also outlives the story.
   Fix: Document voices/previews (and how long it is kept) in privacy.html, or stop caching samples that contain the page's own text, or delete them with the story. Also have make_narration take in or discard voice.speak's 'original', or document it.
17. Stop (or an FFmpeg failure) leaves the partial export/preview and its .ass file in the story folder (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:679)
   What happens: render() writes <what>.ass and <what>.part.mp4 and removes them only on success. When Stop cancels FFmpeg, or FFmpeg fails, both stay in text_stories/<id> until the next render of that kind or the story's deletion, though the page says 'Stopped. Nothing new was kept.' A partial 1080x1920 export can be large.
   Fix: Wrap the ffmpeg_utils.run call in try/except BaseException (or finally on failure) that unlinks tmp and the .ass file before re-raising.
18. Pronunciations whose written word starts or ends with punctuation (Dr., C++) are saved but never applied (clipfoundry/makers/voice_styles.py:120)
   What happens: pronounce() compares the rule's lower-cased words with each token after _core() strips edge punctuation, but the rule's own word keeps its punctuation. 'Dr.' is compared as 'dr.' with 'dr', and 'C++' as 'c++' with 'c'. clean_rules accepts and saves such rules, and the page counts them, but the voice still reads the word as written.
   Fix: Normalise rule words the same way as the text (_core of each rule word), or also match the whole lower-cased token, or reject or strip edge punctuation in clean_rules and tell the owner.
19. A short looping background freezes once 80 loops have played, instead of looping to the end (clipfoundry/makers/textstory.py:576)
   What happens: pieces() stops at MAX_PIECES = 80 inputs. A clip trimmed or uploaded at 1-2 s with 'Loop to the end' (allowed: MIN_PIECE_S = 1) covers only 80-160 s. Stories run up to about 223 s at speed 1.0 and about 318 s at 0.7, and render() then holds the last frame for the rest (tpad=stop_mode=clone). The page promises 'Loop to the end'.
   Fix: Loop one input instead of opening the file up to 80 times: `-stream_loop -1 -ss in -i path` with trim=duration, or a loop filter. Or work out the number of pieces from total/length.
20. A scene PATCH that sends `cast` together with any other change writes the cast list into the scene's picture history, and the short can no longer be opened (HTTP 500) (clipfoundry/makers/shorts.py:1758)
   What happens: update_scene keeps the owner's chosen picture in a local called `chosen` (line 1705: `chosen: dict | None = None # the owner's own picture`). Batch 4 reuses that same name for who is in the shot (line 1758: `chosen = None if body["cast"] is None else cast.scene_members(short, body["cast"])`). When the same PATCH also carries a real change (narration, picture_prompt, move, picture_asset_id, new_picture, again), put() runs `if chosen is not None: out["pictures"] = (list(row.get("pictures") or []) + [chosen])` (lines 1791-1792). That appends the list of member ids, or `[]`, to short_scenes.pictures. From then on, _scene_view's `e.get("asset_id")` raises AttributeError on every GET /api/studio/videos/{id}, so the editor page cannot open the short again. When `cast` comes with `picture_asset_id`, the owner's uploaded picture is also never recorded in the history. ShortCast.tsx always sends `cast` on its own, so the page does not trigger this, but the documented API does ('A scene's PATCH also takes hold_ms, cast ...').
   Fix: Give the 'who is in the shot' value its own name (for example `in_shot = ...`) so it no longer overwrites `chosen`, the picture-history entry. Also add a test that sends cast together with narration in one PATCH.
21. Applying a 'Describe a change' proposal to one shot that puts the usual look back does nothing, but the page says the shot changed (clipfoundry/makers/shorts.py:1997)
   What happens: Shot 3 has a one-shot change (Mia clothing 'a red raincoat'). The owner picks 'Mia as in shot 3', describes 'give her back her yellow raincoat', and gets the proposal {clothing: 'a yellow raincoat and green boots'}. cast_propose computed that proposal against the shot's version. cast_apply, however, filters the changes against the short's base member: `fields, kept = cast.clean_changes(m, changes)`, and clean_changes keeps only `if text != (m.get(f) or "")` (cast.py:360). The value equals the base, so fields is {}, and `if not fields: return {"changed": [], ...}` returns before the scene's override is touched. The red raincoat stays, no build runs, and ShortCast.tsx still shows the toast 'Mia changed in shot 3. ClipForge makes those pictures again.' and closes the panel.
   Fix: For a one-shot apply, compare the changes with the member as that shot shows it (base plus overrides), the same starting point cast_propose uses. Then the put() step that already drops values equal to the base clears the override. Have the page say 'nothing changed' when applied.changed is empty.
22. A dissolve or fade length is worked out only once, so a scene that later gets shorter makes every build fail with 'A transition is longer than half of the shortest clip' (clipfoundry/makers/shorts.py:1338)
   What happens: transition_for() keeps the transition no longer than a third of the shortest clip. assemble() calls it only on the first build, or when the owner changes 'Between scenes' (lines 1338-1340: `if (base is None and short.get("transition", "cut") != "cut") or int(want.get("transition") or 0) > int(applied.get("transition") or 0): plan["transition"] = transition_for(short, clips)`). Later the owner shortens a scene's words, adds or splits a scene, or lowers a hold. The old ms (up to 320) stays in the plan, engine.validate refuses it, and save_plan raises. The short goes to 'failed' and stays there on every later Make until the owner happens to change 'Between scenes'. The error tells them to 'shorten it', but the short's page has no control for the transition length.
   Fix: When short.transition is not 'cut', call transition_for(short, clips) on every assemble, or at least whenever the current plan transition no longer fits the clips. That keeps the ms at no more than a third of the shortest shown clip, and falls back to a cut when it is too short.
23. Privacy policy says a library character can be deleted from the library, but the app has no control that does it, so saved reference photos stay forever (docs/legal/privacy.html:452)
   What happens: privacy.html (lines 452-455) says a character or place kept in the library, with its reference pictures, 'stays in the data folder for your other shorts until you delete it from the library'. project_delete.named_elsewhere then protects those reference pictures while the library names them. The frontend defines shorts.libraryDelete and shorts.libraryChange (frontend/src/shorts.ts:197-199), but nothing calls them. ShortCast.tsx only reads the library (shorts.library()) and adds from it. An owner who saved a character, for example with photos of a real person as references, cannot remove the entry or its pictures from the app. Deleting the short does not remove them either, by design. Only a raw DELETE /api/studio/cast/{id} does.
   Fix: Add a Delete button, and ideally Edit, to the 'From my library' list in ShortCast.tsx that calls shorts.libraryDelete, with a confirmation. Or change the privacy text and docs to say how the library can actually be cleared.
24. Removing a good reference picture fails with a 404 while another of the character's reference pictures is missing (clipfoundry/makers/cast.py:130)
   What happens: A character has refs [A, B] and B's file is gone, so the page shows it as 'Missing'. Pressing Remove on A makes ShortCast.tsx send PATCH .../cast/{member} {refs: [B]}. clean() checks every remaining ref (`for r in refs: if _image_ok(r, workspace_id) is None: raise CastError("A reference picture must be one of your pictures (PNG, JPEG or WebP).", 404)`), so the request is refused with a misleading message, and A cannot be removed until B is removed first.

25. Docs say a scene's hold time changes 'the plan only', but it re-renders the scene's clip, and with Animate the pictures it runs the video model again (docs/clipforge/ANIMATED_SHORTS.md:48)
   What happens: ANIMATED_SHORTS.md item 10 says: "each scene's Stays after the words (0 to 3 s) and the short's Between scenes (cut, dissolve, fade) change the plan only; pictures and voices are not made again." But clip_key includes the hold (shorts.py:571-574: `_hash("clip", ..., END_MS, *((hold,) if hold else ()))`), and _make_scene makes the clip with `duration_s = meta["duration_s"] + (END_MS + hold_ms(scene)) / 1000`. So changing the hold makes the scene's clip again. With motion 'animate' that means another video-model run. The batch's own test asserts that the clip changes (`first["clip_asset_id"] != clip_before`).
   Fix: Change the doc to say that a new hold time makes that scene's clip again (with Animate the pictures, the video maker runs again), while its picture and voice stay. Only the transition is plan-only.
